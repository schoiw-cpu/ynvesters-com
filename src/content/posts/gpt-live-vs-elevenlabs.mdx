## What GPT-Live Actually Is

According to [OpenAI's announcement](https://openai.com/index/), GPT-Live is "a new generation of voice models for natural human-AI interaction," and it started rolling out inside ChatGPT on July 8. As of the follow-up, OpenAI says it is **fully rolled out to all ChatGPT users on the Go, Plus, and Pro plans**, with the free-tier rollout in progress.

The headline technical claim is a **full-duplex architecture** — in OpenAI's words, the model "can listen and speak at the same time." That's what makes the demo feel different from the older ChatGPT Voice: you can interrupt it, it maintains a better sense of timing, and the back-and-forth feels less like walkie-talkie turns and more like a phone call. OpenAI also says that for questions needing web search, deeper reasoning, or more complex work, GPT-Live can **delegate to a frontier model behind the scenes** — so the fast voice layer sits in front, and the heavy thinking happens when it's needed.

For developers, OpenAI's dev team says **GPT-Live-1 and GPT-Live-1 mini are coming to the API soon**, with a sign-up form for notification — but API pricing has not been announced yet, so treat any specific per-minute number you see as speculation until OpenAI publishes it.

Here's the crucial thing that gets lost in the launch-day noise: **GPT-Live is a conversationalist.** It's built for you to *talk with* an assistant. That is not the same product category as a tool built for you to *produce audio* — which is exactly where ElevenLabs lives.

## The Distinction That Decides Everything

![Two different jobs, not one fight: GPT-Live vs ElevenLabs](/images/gpt-live-vs-elevenlabs-card-1.png)

**GPT-Live is for talking to an AI.** Brainstorming out loud on a walk, practicing a language, hands-free Q&A, a natural voice interface for ChatGPT. The output is a live conversation. You don't get a clean audio file you can drop into a video timeline, sell to a client, or publish as an audiobook chapter.

**ElevenLabs is for making audio you keep.** Narration, dubbing, voice cloning, text-to-speech for a product, a branded voice for a podcast intro. The output is a downloadable, licensable asset — the thing that actually appears in a deliverable someone pays you for.

Ask yourself one question and the answer falls out immediately: **do I want to have a conversation, or do I want to produce a file?** If it's a conversation, GPT-Live is right there in the plan you probably already pay for. If it's a file you'll edit, ship, or sell, GPT-Live doesn't do that job — and buying a ChatGPT plan won't get you there.

## When GPT-Live Is Genuinely Enough

Be honest about how you actually use voice AI, because for a lot of people GPT-Live quietly covers it:

- You use ChatGPT Voice to think out loud, ask questions hands-free, or rubber-duck a problem. GPT-Live is a straight upgrade, and it's included — nothing to cancel, nothing to add.
- You were paying for a separate voice tool *only* to have better spoken conversations with an assistant. That's now redundant with what's in your ChatGPT plan.
- You're a casual user who never actually exports audio. You don't need a production tool at all.

If that's you, our take is simple: **use GPT-Live, and don't pay for a second voice subscription you were only using to chat.** The most expensive subscription is the one you forgot you're not really using.

You're deciding whether you need a *conversation* (already in your ChatGPT plan) or a *production asset* (what ElevenLabs charges for). Paying for ElevenLabs only makes sense if you produce audio — but if you do, GPT-Live's arrival changes nothing about that need.

## FAQ

**Does GPT-Live replace ElevenLabs?**
No. GPT-Live is a real-time conversational voice model inside ChatGPT; ElevenLabs produces downloadable audio assets like narration, dubbing, and voice clones. Different jobs.

**Is GPT-Live free?**
It's included in ChatGPT's Go, Plus, and Pro plans, with free-tier rollout in progress per OpenAI. There's no separate GPT-Live subscription; API pricing hasn't been announced.

**Can I use GPT-Live to make a voiceover for a video?**
That's not the use case OpenAI announced — GPT-Live is built for conversation, not for generating and exporting audio files. For voiceovers, a production tool like ElevenLabs is the right fit.

**Should I cancel my voice subscription now that GPT-Live is out?**
Only if you were using it purely to have spoken conversations with an assistant. If you produce or sell audio, keep the production tool — GPT-Live doesn't do that job.
