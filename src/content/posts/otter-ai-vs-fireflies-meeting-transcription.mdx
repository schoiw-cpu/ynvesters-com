Remote and hybrid work has made AI meeting transcription non-negotiable. Whether you're running a 12-person sprint review or a 200-attendee all-hands, manually note-taking is a liability. Two tools dominate this category in 2026: [Otter.ai](https://otter.ai) and [Fireflies.ai](https://fireflies.ai).

Both record, transcribe, and summarize meetings. Both integrate with Zoom, Google Meet, and Microsoft Teams. Both cost roughly the same at entry level. But the differences between them are real, consequential, and often overlooked in generic comparisons.

This breakdown focuses on the features that actually matter for working professionals — not the marketing page bullet points.

---

## 1. Transcription Accuracy

Transcription quality is the foundation. A tool that mishears "revenue" as "review" in a financial review is worse than useless.

**Otter.ai** uses its proprietary ASR (Automatic Speech Recognition) engine, optimized heavily for English. In controlled tests with clear audio, Otter consistently delivers accuracy in the 90–95% range for standard American and British English. It struggles more with heavy accents, technical jargon, and overlapping speakers.

**Fireflies.ai** leans on a combination of its own models and third-party ASR infrastructure. Its accuracy is comparable in clean audio conditions, but Fireflies has invested more visibly in multilingual transcription. According to [Fireflies' own documentation](https://fireflies.ai/blog), it supports transcription in over 60 languages as of 2025, while Otter's multilingual support remains more limited.

**Winner:** Fireflies.ai for multilingual teams; Otter.ai for English-primary, accent-light environments.

---

## 2. Real-Time Transcription

Live transcription during a meeting — not just a post-call summary — changes how teams operate. Participants can follow along, catch missed points, and reduce "sorry, can you repeat that?" moments.

**Otter.ai** has made real-time transcription a core differentiator. OtterPilot joins your meeting as a bot and streams transcription live, with speaker labels appearing within seconds of speech. The live transcript is accessible from a browser or the Otter mobile app while the meeting is in progress.

**Fireflies.ai** primarily operates as a post-meeting tool. Its Notetaker bot joins calls and records everything, but the live transcript experience is less polished than Otter's. The focus is on the post-call package — full transcript, AI summary, and action items — delivered after the meeting ends.

**Winner:** Otter.ai, clearly. Real-time transcription is a genuine strength here.

---

## 3. AI Summaries and Action Items

A raw transcript of a 60-minute meeting is rarely what anyone wants to read. AI-generated summaries and extracted action items are now table stakes — but the quality varies enormously.

**Otter.ai** generates an "AI Summary" that includes a brief overview, outline, and a list of action items. The summaries are readable but can be generic for complex meetings. The action item extraction is functional but sometimes misses implicit commitments ("I'll look into that" rarely gets flagged as an action item).

**Fireflies.ai** goes further here. Its "AI Super Summary" includes a structured breakdown: meeting overview, keywords, action items, questions asked, and key topics with timestamps. The question extraction feature is particularly useful for sales teams and researchers. Fireflies also lets users create custom "Smart Filters" to extract specific types of information — pricing discussions, objections, next steps — which Otter does not offer.

**Winner:** Fireflies.ai for structured, customizable AI summaries.

---

## 4. Integrations and Workflow Automation

A transcription tool that lives in isolation is a dead end. The real value comes from pushing meeting data into the tools your team already uses.

**Otter.ai** integrates natively with Zoom, Google Meet, Microsoft Teams, Slack, Salesforce, and HubSpot. Its Zapier connectivity extends this further. The Salesforce and HubSpot integrations allow meeting notes to be pushed directly to CRM records, which matters for sales teams.

**Fireflies.ai** integrates with a significantly wider ecosystem. Native integrations include Zoom, Teams, Meet, Slack, Salesforce, HubSpot, Notion, Linear, Trello, Asana, Monday.com, and more — over 40 native app connections. Fireflies also supports webhooks and has a public API, giving technical teams more flexibility. For teams that run on project management tools, Fireflies' breadth is a practical advantage.

You can explore Fireflies' integration list on their [integrations page](https://fireflies.ai/integrations).

**Winner:** Fireflies.ai for integration depth and API access.

## 6. Search and Knowledge Management

Meeting content is only useful if you can find it later. Search capability determines whether your transcription library becomes an organizational asset or an archive nobody uses.

**Otter.ai** offers keyword search across all transcripts, which is functional for finding specific moments. It also introduced an "AI Chat" feature (available on paid plans) that lets users ask natural language questions across their meeting history — for example, "What did we decide about the Q3 budget?" This is a meaningful step toward turning transcripts into a searchable knowledge base.

**Fireflies.ai** has a feature called "AI Search" that lets users filter across all meetings by topic, speaker, date, and sentiment. The topic-based filtering — where the AI clusters related discussions across multiple meetings — is more sophisticated than simple keyword matching. Teams can also create "Playlists" of key meeting moments, which is useful for onboarding, training, and sharing context with stakeholders who weren't in the original meeting.

**Winner:** Fireflies.ai for search depth and knowledge organization features.

## 7. Privacy, Security, and Compliance

Meetings often contain sensitive information — personnel discussions, financial data, client details, legal conversations. How a tool handles this data matters.

**Otter.ai** stores data on AWS infrastructure in the United States. It offers SOC 2 Type 2 compliance and provides admin controls for data retention and deletion. Business and Enterprise plans include additional security features like SSO (Single Sign-On) and custom data retention policies. Otter's privacy policy is available at [otter.ai/privacy](https://otter.ai/privacy).

**Fireflies.ai** is also SOC 2 Type 2 certified and stores data on AWS. It offers GDPR compliance documentation and provides data residency options for Enterprise customers. Fireflies also allows users to delete specific meeting recordings and transcripts independently, which is a useful granularity that some compliance teams require.

Both tools allow meeting participants to be notified when a bot joins — a legal requirement in many jurisdictions under two-party consent laws. If you're in California, Illinois, or anywhere in the EU, understanding how consent notifications work in your chosen tool is not optional. The [GDPR official text](https://gdpr.eu/what-is-gdpr/) and local recording consent laws should be reviewed before deployment.

**Winner:** Roughly equal; Fireflies has a slight edge on granular data deletion controls.

## 8. Mobile App Experience

Not everyone is at a desk. Sales reps, field teams, and executives often need to capture and review meeting notes on the go.

**Otter.ai** has a polished iOS and Android app with a long track record. The app supports live transcription from your phone's microphone (useful for in-person meetings without a laptop), playback with synchronized transcript highlighting, and sharing capabilities. It's one of the more mature mobile experiences in this category.

**Fireflies.ai** has a mobile app, but it's primarily designed for reviewing and accessing meeting content rather than capturing in-person meetings through the phone's microphone. The app is functional but trails Otter's in terms of live capture capability and overall polish.

**Winner:** Otter.ai, notably for in-person meeting capture on mobile.

---

## 9. Team Collaboration Features

Transcription tools increasingly double as collaboration layers. How teams interact with meeting content collectively is a growing differentiator.

**Otter.ai** supports collaborative note-taking where multiple team members can add comments, highlight sections, and assign action items within a shared transcript. The "Channels" feature lets teams organize meetings by project or group, and shared workspaces allow collective access to transcripts. This positions Otter as a lightweight team wiki for meeting content.

**Fireflies.ai** includes commenting and reaction features on transcripts, plus the Playlist functionality mentioned earlier for curating highlight reels. Its team analytics dashboard (available on Business plans) shows metrics like meeting frequency, talk-time ratios, and topic trends across the organization — data that can be genuinely useful for managers and team leads. This analytics layer is something Otter does not currently match.

**Winner:** Fireflies.ai for team analytics; Otter.ai for collaborative note-taking UX.

## What Head-to-Head Accuracy Testing Shows

Independent testing in 2026 confirms that transcription accuracy between these two tools is remarkably close — with one meaningful exception.

In a **head-to-head test of 12 identical meetings with the same audio input**, **Fireflies achieved 94.2% word-level accuracy** while **Otter scored 93.8%**, per [Smart Tools Pick's 20-meeting comparison](https://smarttoolspick.com/fireflies-vs-otter-ai-2026/). The 0.4% gap was characterized as statistical noise in standard English-language conditions — on a 500-word transcript, that's roughly two words different.

Both tools perform similarly well in clean conditions: on calls with native English speakers and decent audio, **both hit 92–96% accuracy** consistently, per [Get Alfred's 2026 notetaker review](https://get-alfred.ai/blog/fireflies-vs-otter). The difference opens in two challenging scenarios: with **accents and background noise, neither tool crosses 85% reliably**, and the advantage shifts to whichever tool has better pre-processing for your specific audio environment.

The clearest differentiator found in testing: **when two people talk over each other (crosstalk), Fireflies maintains speaker label accuracy better than Otter**, which tends to merge overlapping speech into a single speaker attribution — a meaningful issue for multi-participant sales calls or panel meetings, per [The Business Dive's real comparison](https://thebusinessdive.com/fireflies-ai-vs-otter-ai).

If raw accuracy on clean recordings is your primary metric, either tool will serve you equally well. The real differentiation is in the workflows built around the transcript.

## Conclusion

Neither Otter.ai nor Fireflies.ai is universally better. The right choice depends on where your team's workflow actually breaks down.

**Choose Otter.ai** if real-time transcription and mobile meeting capture are non-negotiable, or if your team is English-first and values a cleaner interface over feature density.

**Choose Fireflies.ai** if you need multilingual support, want a broader integration ecosystem, or are running a sales or research team that needs structured AI summaries, custom filters, and cross-meeting analytics.

For most mid-sized or enterprise teams with global reach and multiple SaaS tools in their stack, **Fireflies.ai is the stronger default choice in 2026**. For individual contributors, coaches, journalists, or smaller English-speaking teams, **Otter.ai's real-time experience and mobile polish earn it the edge**.

Both tools offer free tiers — the fastest way to make this decision is to run both on the same meeting type for two weeks and compare the output quality directly. The transcripts will tell you everything you need to know.
